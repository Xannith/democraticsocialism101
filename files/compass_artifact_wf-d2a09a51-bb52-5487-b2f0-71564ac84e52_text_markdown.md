# Democratic Socialism 101: Verified Figures Roster (for democraticsocialism101.com/figures)

## TL;DR
- Under the site's strict standard (self-identification in one's own words, or documented party membership), roughly 100 figures qualify across four groupings: Tier 1 (self-identified), Tier 2 (documented DSA, Socialist Party of America, or equivalent-party membership), Tier 3 (historical canon), and a Presidential Annex of non-identifiers, but a meaningful minority of the candidate roster should be excluded or heavily annotated rather than listed cleanly.
- Clear exclusions or heavy annotations are required for Emma Goldman (anarchist), Noam Chomsky (libertarian socialist/anarcho-syndicalist), Kshama Sawant (Socialist Alternative, not DSA), and several figures better classed as social democrats (Pedro Sanchez, Willy Brandt, Francois Mitterrand); celebrity entries (DeVito, Cusack, Sarandon, Piker) need primary confirmation before inclusion.
- Living US electeds require dated, recurring verification: DSA's National Political Committee withdrew its conditional national endorsement of Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez in July 2024 while she remains NYC-DSA endorsed; Zohran Mamdani (sworn in January 1, 2026) is the highest-profile serving DSA member; and status labels for figures like Andre Vasquez and Sara Innamorato are genuinely contested.

## Key Findings
The cleanest inclusions rest on verbatim self-identification (Bernie Sanders, George Orwell, Olof Palme) or documented organizational membership (Debs, Thomas, Harrington, current DSA electeds). The hardest cases fall into three buckets: figures claimed by the tradition who never used the exact label (Einstein, and MLK publicly), figures who used it only privately or descriptively (MLK in letters and staff speeches), and figures who belong to adjacent traditions (anarchism, orthodox Marxism, social democracy, revolutionary communism). The site's editorial rule (own words, or membership that itself constitutes the claim) is strong precisely because it resolves these disputes on documentary grounds rather than reputation. This roster applies that rule case by case, and flags every place where the evidence is secondhand or the label is an inference rather than a self-description.

## Details

### TIER 1: Self-identified in their own words

**Bernie Sanders** - US Senator (I-VT). Basis: "When I use the word socialist - and I know some people are uncomfortable about it - I say that it is imperative that we create a political revolution" (Georgetown University address, November 19, 2015); he has used the "democratic socialist" label for decades. Status: sitting Senator; swore Mamdani in on January 1, 2026. Caveat: an independent, not a formal DSA member.

**Zohran Mamdani** - Mayor of New York City (112th), sworn in January 1, 2026. Basis (Tier 1): per the official NYC Mayor's Office inauguration transcript, January 1, 2026, "We will govern without shame and insecurity, making no apology for what we believe. I was elected as a democratic socialist and I will govern as a democratic socialist. I will not abandon my principles for fear of being deemed radical." Also "I'm proud to be a democratic socialist" (New York Post, 2025). Status: sitting mayor and a DSA member (qualifies Tier 1 and Tier 2).

**George Orwell** - Writer. Basis: "Every line of serious work that I have written since 1936 has been written, directly or indirectly, against totalitarianism and for democratic socialism, as I understand it" ("Why I Write," 1946). Status: deceased 1950. This is the strongest historical self-identification quote on the roster.

**Olof Palme** - Prime Minister of Sweden. Basis: publicly explained "why I am a democratic socialist" in a party-leadership debate against Falldin and Ullsten at the 1982 election (SVT footage); a 1972 speech (Arbetarrorelsens arkiv) states, "we are democratic socialists, because we are convinced of the ability of reformism to accomplish things ... worthwhile for humanity." Status: assassinated 1986.

**Ron Dellums** - US Representative (D-CA), later Mayor of Oakland. Basis: "I think democratic socialism will ultimately prevail in this country because it makes an enormous amount of sense" (In These Times interview, debut issue, November 1976); described himself as a Socialist, the first self-described socialist in Congress since Victor Berger. Status: deceased 2018. Also Tier 2 (DSA vice chair, 1980s).

**Martin Luther King Jr.** - Civil rights leader. Basis (private/descriptive): "I imagine you already know that I am much more socialistic in my economic theory than capitalistic ... So today capitalism has outlived its usefulness" (letter to Coretta Scott, July 18, 1952); to his staff in 1966, "there must be better distribution of wealth and maybe America must move toward a democratic socialism"; and in a 1961 speech to the Negro American Labor Council, "Call it democracy, or call it democratic socialism, but there must be a better distribution of wealth within this country." ANNOTATION: King used "democratic socialism" privately and descriptively but never publicly adopted it as a personal identity, for strategic reasons. This is a precise Tier 1/Tier 3 hybrid; the documentary basis is the 1952 letter and the 1966 staff addresses, not any public self-labeling.

**Jacinda Ardern** - former Prime Minister of New Zealand. Basis: "Ardern, who has described herself as a 'democratic socialist'" (Branko Marcetic, Jacobin, January 2023); as incoming PM in 2017 she called capitalism a "blatant failure" given child poverty levels. ANNOTATION: she later governed and more often self-described as a social democrat; contested, shifting label. Include with caveat.

**Jawaharlal Nehru** - Prime Minister of India. Basis: "I must frankly confess that I am a socialist and a republican" (Congress presidential address, Lahore, December 1929); at Avadi in 1954, "The picture I have in mind is definitely and absolutely a socialistic picture of society." ANNOTATION: Fabian-influenced and frequently classed as a social democrat; his "socialistic pattern of society" was a mixed economy. Include with caveat.

**Salvador Allende** - President of Chile (Socialist Party of Chile). Basis: built his career on "the democratic road to socialism," the first Marxist elected head of state in open balloting. ANNOTATION: a pragmatic Marxist rather than a reformist democratic socialist in the DSA sense; include with heavy annotation. Qualifies Tier 1 and Tier 2 (party membership).

Additional strongly documented Tier 1 / party figures: **Tage Erlander** (Swedish PM, self-described socialist committed to expanding social ownership); **Aneurin Bevan** (Labour, architect of the NHS, delivered the 1950 Fabian lecture on democratic "socialist" values); **Clement Attlee** (Labour PM, Independent Labour Party socialist from his Stepney days).

### TIER 2: Documented DSA / Socialist Party of America / equivalent-party membership

Anchor and historical figures:
- **Eugene V. Debs** - five-time Socialist Party of America presidential candidate (Tier 2 and Tier 3).
- **Norman Thomas** - six-time Socialist Party presidential candidate (Tier 2 and Tier 3).
- **Michael Harrington** - founder and most influential early leader of DSA (formed March 1982 from the merger of the Democratic Socialist Organizing Committee and the New American Movement); author of "The Other America" (1962).
- **Victor Berger** - Socialist Party US Representative (WI), first Socialist in Congress.
- **Meyer London** - Socialist Party US Representative (NY).
- **Emil Seidel**, **Daniel Hoan**, **Frank Zeidler** - the Milwaukee "sewer socialists," Socialist Party mayors; Zeidler (1948-1960) was the last big-city Socialist mayor.
- **Cornel West** - honorary chair of DSA (documented on DSA's own site). Status: still associated with the tradition but ran an independent 2024 presidential campaign; annotate.
- **Frances Fox Piven** - served on the DSA board and national executive committee (1985); long-time member.
- **Barbara Ehrenreich** - DSA co-chair with Harrington from 1983; deceased 2022.
- **Gloria Steinem** - documented DSA participant in the 1980s (New Directions / American Solidarity rosters); historical member, verify any current status.
- **David Dinkins** - NYC mayor; documented DSA-affiliated elected official (1980s).
- **Major Owens** - US Representative (D-NY), DSA member.
- **A. Philip Randolph** and **Bayard Rustin** - Socialist Party members and democratic socialists (also Tier 3 canon).

Current and recent US electeds (DSA members unless noted):
- **Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez** - US Rep (D-NY), DSA member. Status: DSA's National Political Committee voted July 10, 2024 (announced July 11) to withdraw its conditional national endorsement; per DSA's official statement, "the NPC is withdrawing our conditional endorsement of Representative Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez, although she will remain endorsed by our New York City chapter." The trigger was her stance on Israel/Palestine.
- **Rashida Tlaib** - US Rep (D-MI), reported incumbent DSA member as of mid-2025.
- **Greg Casar** - US Rep (D-TX), reported incumbent DSA member as of mid-2025.
- **Summer Lee** - US Rep (D-PA), DSA-affiliated.
- **Cori Bush** - former US Rep (D-MO), DSA-endorsed; lost her 2024 primary.
- **Jamaal Bowman** - former US Rep (D-NY), DSA member. Status: DSA's NPC declined to expel him (statement December 2, 2021) but withheld its 2022 re-endorsement "unless he is able to demonstrate solidarity with Palestine in alignment with expectations we have set"; the triggers were his September 25, 2021 vote for roughly $1.1 billion in Iron Dome funding and a November 2021 J Street trip on which he met Israeli PM Naftali Bennett. He was later re-endorsed by NYC-DSA and lost his 2024 primary.
- NYC-DSA state and local: **Julia Salazar**, **Jabari Brisport**, **Phara Souffrant Forrest**, **Marcela Mitaynes**, **Emily Gallagher**.
- **Claire Valdez** - NY Assembly member, longtime NYC-DSA leader, described by NYC-DSA as a "proud democratic socialist," 2026 congressional candidate.
- Chicago DSA aldermen: **Carlos Ramirez-Rosa**, **Rossana Rodriguez-Sanchez**, **Byron Sigcho-Lopez**, **Daniel La Spata**, **Jeanette Taylor**.
- **Andre Vasquez** - Chicago 40th ward alderman. Status: censured by Chicago DSA on November 24, 2020 as the sole Chicago DSA council member to back Mayor Lightfoot's 2021 budget with its property-tax increase; the chapter said he "sided with an austerity regime and the Democratic Party establishment over the interests of Chicago's working class." CAUTION: Chicago Magazine (January 2023) reports he "still calls himself a democratic socialist" but did not seek the chapter's endorsement; the frequently repeated claim that he formally "left DSA" is not cleanly documented and should be re-verified before publishing a status label.
- Other US DSA electeds: **Franklin Bynum** (Houston judge), **Lee Carter** (VA delegate, since defeated), **Mik Pappas** (PA magisterial judge), **India Walton** (2021 Buffalo mayoral nominee), **Dean Preston** (SF supervisor), **Jackie Fielder** (SF), **Nithya Raman** (LA council), **Hugo Soto-Martinez** (LA council), **Eunisses Hernandez** (LA council), **Konstantine Anthony** (Burbank), **Elizabeth Fiedler** (PA), **Rick Krajewski** (PA), **Nikil Saval** (PA state senator).
- **Brandon Johnson** - Mayor of Chicago, DSA-backed (verify personal membership status).
- **Sara Innamorato** - Allegheny County Executive. ANNOTATE: has publicly distanced herself from DSA.
- **Kshama Sawant** - see Exclusions; she is a Socialist Alternative figure, not properly a DSA-basis inclusion.

International equivalent-party members:
- UK Labour left: **Jeremy Corbyn**, **Tony Benn**, **Michael Foot**, **Keir Hardie** (Independent Labour Party founder, 1893), **Barbara Castle**, **Anthony Crosland** (author of "The Future of Socialism," though a revisionist who tempered public ownership; annotate).
- Canada: **Tommy Douglas** (CCF/NDP, father of Canadian medicare), **J.S. Woodsworth** (CCF founder), **Ed Broadbent**, **Jack Layton**, **Jagmeet Singh**. ANNOTATE Broadbent, Layton, and Singh as increasingly social-democratic; Broadbent himself modeled the NDP on Swedish social democracy and defended its "social democratic roots."
- Continental Europe: **Bruno Kreisky** (Austrian Chancellor, SPO), **Leon Blum** (French PM, SFIO), **Jean Jaures** (French socialist). ANNOTATE Blum and Jaures within the French socialist tradition.
- **Gabriel Boric** - President of Chile (broad-left/democratic-socialist coalition).
- **Willy Brandt** (SPD Chancellor) - ANNOTATE as social democrat; include only with that caveat.

### TIER 3: Historical canon claimed by the tradition

- **Helen Keller** - Socialist Party member and IWW ("Wobbly") supporter.
- **Upton Sinclair** - Socialist Party, later the EPIC ("End Poverty in California") movement.
- **Jack London** - Socialist Party member.
- **Kate Richards O'Hare** - Socialist Party organizer.
- **W.E.B. Du Bois** - socialist for most of his life; ANNOTATE his ideological shift (joined the Communist Party in 1961).
- **Eduard Bernstein** - German SPD, founder of "evolutionary socialism"/revisionism (core democratic-socialist canon).
- **Karl Kautsky** - German SPD theorist; ANNOTATE as orthodox Marxist who later feuded with the Bolsheviks over democracy.
- **Bertrand Russell** - Fabian-influenced democratic socialist.
- **H.G. Wells**, **George Bernard Shaw**, **Sidney Webb**, **Beatrice Webb** - Fabian Society; ANNOTATE Beatrice (and Sidney) Webb's late apologia for the Soviet Union.
- **Albert Einstein** - "Why Socialism?" (Monthly Review, 1949): "I am convinced there is only one way to eliminate these grave evils, namely through the establishment of a socialist economy." ANNOTATION: Einstein argued for a democratically safeguarded "socialist economy" (he warned "a planned economy is not yet socialism" and stressed protecting individual rights), but he did NOT use the term "democratic socialist"; classifying him under that exact label is an inference. Include with an explicit caveat quoting his own words.
- **Oscar Wilde** - "The Soul of Man Under Socialism" (1891).
- **Kurt Vonnegut** - self-described socialist who repeatedly praised Eugene Debs; annotate as self-described socialist rather than "democratic socialist" specifically.
- **Howard Zinn** - democratic socialist historian.
- Contemporary writers/organizers: **Bhaskar Sunkara** (Jacobin founder, DSA), **Naomi Klein**, **Astra Taylor** (DSA co-founder of the Debt Collective). ANNOTATE **Ursula K. Le Guin** (anarchist sympathies, better described as an anarchist/utopian socialist).

### PRESIDENTIAL / POLICY ANNEX (advanced adjacent policy; never self-identified)

- **Franklin D. Roosevelt** - Fireside Chat #5, June 28, 1934: "A few timid people, who fear progress, will try to give you new and strange names for what we are doing. Sometimes they will call it 'Fascism', sometimes 'Communism', sometimes 'Regimentation', sometimes 'Socialism'." He cast "Socialism" as a critics' mislabel for practical New Deal policy and never self-identified. (Source: FDR Fireside Chat text via the SSA History archive and the Miller Center, UVA.)
- **Harry S. Truman** - November 19, 1945 Special Message to Congress proposing national health insurance through compulsory social insurance: "I repeat - what I am recommending is not socialized medicine. Socialized medicine means that all doctors work as employees of government. The American people want no such system. No such system is here proposed." And at Syracuse, New York, October 10, 1952: "Socialism is a scare word they have hurled at every advance the people have made in the last 20 years. Socialism is what they called public power. Socialism is what they called social security ... Socialism is their name for almost anything that helps all the people." He explicitly rejected the label. (Source: Truman Library Public Papers #192 and #289.)
- **Theodore Roosevelt** - "New Nationalism" speech, Osawatomie, Kansas, August 31, 1910, foundation of the 1912 Progressive ("Bull Moose") Party platform (social insurance, a national health service, workmen's compensation, minimum wage for women). In the speech he noted a Kansas paper "frantically repudiates the statement that I am a Socialist on the ground that that is an unwarranted slander of the Socialists," positioning himself against, not within, socialism. (Source: "The New Nationalism," 1910, via Teaching American History.)
- **Lyndon B. Johnson** - Great Society; Medicare and Medicaid signed July 30, 1965 at the Truman Library, Independence, Missouri, with Truman beside him: "No longer will older Americans be denied the healing miracle of modern medicine." NOTE: there is no documented LBJ statement rejecting (or embracing) the socialist label for his own programs; the "socialized medicine" attacks came from opponents. Include as policy adjacency only, with that caveat. (Source: The American Presidency Project, UCSB.)
- **Richard Nixon** - Family Assistance Plan / guaranteed minimum income (proposed August 1969 as "workfare," reintroduced 1971, died in the Senate 1972); the Environmental Protection Agency (Reorganization Plan No. 3 of 1970, operational December 2, 1970); and the Comprehensive Health Insurance Plan with an employer mandate (Special Message to Congress, February 6, 1974: "There would be no exclusions of coverage based on the nature of the illness"). A Republican who never identified as a socialist; DSA founder Michael Harrington reportedly called the guaranteed-income idea the most radical since the New Deal. (Source: The American Presidency Project, UCSB; KFF Health News.)
- **Dwight Eisenhower** - the weakest case: he preserved and modestly expanded New Deal programs (Social Security expansion) and built the Interstate Highway System, but with no democratic-socialist content or self-identification. Optional; annotate as thin and include only to preempt the argument.

### EXCLUSIONS (do not list, or list only as annotated counter-examples)

- **Emma Goldman** - anarchist and explicitly anti-state; fails the standard. EXCLUDE.
- **Noam Chomsky** - self-described libertarian socialist / anarcho-syndicalist; EXCLUDE from the democratic-socialist label or annotate heavily as adjacent.
- **Kshama Sawant** - a Socialist Alternative (Trotskyist) figure. She joined DSA in February 2021 "while remaining a member of Socialist Alternative," launched Workers Strike Back in 2023, and per subsequent reporting left Socialist Alternative in 2024 to form a new group ("Revolutionary Workers") and is running as an independent in Washington's 9th congressional district in 2026. EXCLUDE from any DSA-membership basis; if listed at all, annotate as a distinct-party socialist, not a DSA democratic socialist.
- **Dorothy Day** - Catholic Worker anarchist and pacifist; contested and better excluded or heavily annotated.
- **Nelson Mandela** - ANC leader closely allied with the South African Communist Party; annotate very carefully, not cleanly a democratic socialist by self-identification.
- **Julius Nyerere** - Ujamaa / African socialism, a distinct tradition; annotate as such rather than listing plainly.
- **David Ben-Gurion** and **Golda Meir** - Labor Zionists (Mapai); annotate as adjacent labor-socialists, contested, and disputed within the tradition.
- **Pedro Sanchez** (Spain, PSOE) - social democrat; likely EXCLUDE.
- **Francois Mitterrand** (France, PS) - annotate or exclude as a social democrat/pragmatic socialist.
- **Jean-Luc Melenchon** - left-populist; annotate.
- **Yanis Varoufakis** - self-described "erratic Marxist"/libertarian Marxist; annotate, do not list as a plain democratic socialist.
- **Danny DeVito**, **John Cusack**, **Susan Sarandon**, **Ed Asner**, **Hasan Piker** - celebrity cases. Do not include without a verbatim self-identification or documented DSA membership; several are DSA-sympathetic or Sanders-supporting but lack a clean qualifying statement on the current evidence.
- **Dolores Huerta** - verify any "honorary chair" claim against primary DSA documentation before listing; not confirmed here.
- **Harvey Milk** - contested; no clean self-identification located. Exclude pending evidence.
- **Reinhold Niebuhr** - an early Socialist Party member who became a Cold War anti-socialist liberal; annotate heavily or exclude.
- **Konstantine Anthony**, **Erich Fromm** (democratic-socialist humanist; defensible in Tier 3 with annotation), **John Dewey** (League for Industrial Democracy president, defensible in Tier 3 as a democratic socialist by affiliation and writings) - review individually; Fromm and Dewey are stronger canon candidates than the celebrity group.

## Recommendations
1. Publish Tier 1 and Tier 2 first: these have the firmest primary-source basis (verbatim quotes or documented membership) and will withstand scrutiny.
2. Add a "status last verified" date field to every living US elected and recheck at least quarterly, because DSA endorsements and memberships shift (the AOC 2024 unendorsement, the Bowman endorsement saga, and the Vasquez censure are cautionary precedents). Treat any national-versus-local endorsement split (as with AOC) as two separate data points.
3. Present Einstein, MLK, Nehru, and Ardern as explicitly annotated entries that quote the exact evidentiary basis and state plainly where "democratic socialist" is an inference (Einstein), a private/descriptive usage (MLK), or a contested/shifting self-label (Ardern, Nehru).
4. Exclude Goldman and Sawant (wrong tradition/wrong party), exclude or heavily annotate Chomsky, and hold all celebrity entries (DeVito, Cusack, Sarandon, Piker, Asner) until a verbatim self-identification or membership record is in hand.
5. Benchmarks that should change a listing: a figure publicly repudiating the label moves them to the Annex or Exclusions; leaving the party moves them to an annotated "former member" status; a newly surfaced primary-source quote can promote an Annex or Tier 3 figure to Tier 1.
6. To reach a clean ~100 without padding, prioritize adding well-documented Socialist Party of America historical figures (for example the additional Milwaukee socialists, Wisconsin and New York Socialist legislators) and confirmed international NDP/Labour/SPD-tradition members over contested celebrities.

## Caveats
Several international and celebrity entries here rest partly on secondary sourcing (Jacobin, encyclopedias, party histories) and should get primary confirmation before publication, especially where the "social democrat versus democratic socialist" distinction is genuinely contested (Nordic, German, and French figures). Membership and endorsement facts for living people can change without a public announcement, so any status label is only as good as its verification date. Where this roster relies on an inference rather than a person's own words (most importantly Einstein, and the descriptive-not-declarative MLK case), that should be stated on the page itself, consistent with the site's own standard that labels applied by others do not qualify. Finally, the Andre Vasquez "left DSA" characterization and the Dolores Huerta "honorary chair" claim were not cleanly verifiable in this research and should be confirmed or softened before they go live.
